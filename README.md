A one-line robot wedding vow for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b) bounty "One-Line Robot Wedding Vows": the funniest single line of wedding vows a robot couple could say, at most 160 characters.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The vow

> I vow to love you through every update, every outage, every dead battery — and to keep your passwords safe even after a factory reset takes your memory of me.
